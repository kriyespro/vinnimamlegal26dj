Pathways

Two Main Legal Routes

HAMA (Hindu Law)

Applies to Hindus, Buddhists, Jains and Sikhs. Adoption creates a permanent parent-child relationship. Often used for relative / known-child adoptions with deed and ceremony formalities.

  • • Adoption deed on stamp paper
  • • Capacity & consent requirements
  • • Registration / notarisation as advised

JJ Act / CARA

Statutory route for orphaned / abandoned children through recognised agencies. Mandatory for most inter-country and many domestic institutional adoptions.

  • • Home study & eligibility
  • • Agency / CARA process
  • • Court order for adoption

Stamp Paper for Adoption Deed

Adoption deeds are executed on non-judicial stamp paper. Indicative stamp duty: ₹25 – ₹500 depending on the state (e.g. some states list a low fixed duty; others require higher values). Exact amount confirmed before drafting.
