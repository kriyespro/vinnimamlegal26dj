FAQ

GST Questions Answered

Businesses with an aggregate annual turnover exceeding ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs for special category states) are mandatorily required to register for GST. Certain businesses like e-commerce operators and inter-state suppliers must register regardless of turnover.
A regular taxpayer must file GSTR-1 (monthly/quarterly), GSTR-3B (monthly), and GSTR-9 (annual). Some businesses may also need to file GSTR-2B reconciliation and GSTR-9C audit certificate if their turnover exceeds ₹2 crores.
Late filing of GSTR-3B attracts a late fee of ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), capped at ₹10,000 per return. For nil returns, the late fee is ₹20 per day. Delayed payment of tax also attracts interest at 18% p.a.
Yes, registered businesses can claim ITC on goods and services used for business purposes. ITC is visible in GSTR-2B based on supplier filings. Our team reconciles your purchase register with GSTR-2B to maximize your eligible ITC claims.
