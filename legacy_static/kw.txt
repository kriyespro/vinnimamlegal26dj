LexAssist — Portal Usage Policy (source notes)
=============================================
Clean published page: portal-usage-policy.html
Working portal UI: portal.html

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
Governs use of the Firm's legal services portal by:
- Office / Back-End Staff
- Partners / Advocates
- Clients / Customers

Laws: DPDP Act 2023, IT Act 2000, Bar Council of India Rules.
Access is conditional on accepting this policy.

2. OFFICE / BACK-END STAFF
2.1 Access & Confidentiality
- Access only assigned case files / client data
- Never share client info outside Firm without written authorization
- Credentials personal — never share
- Revoke access immediately on transfer/exit

2.2 Conduct
- Document all client communication (calls, emails, meetings) in the case file

2.3 Data Handling
- No downloads to personal devices / email / cloud
- All uploads/edits/deletes logged (user ID, timestamp, action)
- Report breach/phishing/unauthorised access to Compliance/IT immediately

2.4 Escalation
- Legal advice / opinion / settlement → escalate to Partner; staff must not answer

3. PARTNERS / ADVOCATES
3.1 Case Ownership
- Partner responsible for assigned cases; timely portal status updates
- Major docs reviewed & approved by Partner before client share

3.2 Confidentiality & Conflict
- Disclose conflicts before accepting new case/client
- Need-to-know sharing between Partners
- Follow BCI Rules on confidentiality & ethics

3.3 Portal Usage
- Update case status, hearing dates, billing regularly
- Review staff drafts within 24–48 hours

3.4 Financial
- Transparent fees consistent with client communication
- Fee changes via writing/portal before they take effect

4. CLIENTS / CUSTOMERS
4.1 Access
- View-only / limited to own cases; never other clients' data
- OTP or secure login before sensitive documents

4.2 Communication
- Formal instructions/queries via portal (not only WhatsApp/calls)
- Response times within business days

4.3 Document Submission
- Requested formats only (PDF, JPEG), within size limits
- Client responsible for accuracy & authenticity

4.4 Fees & Payments
- Payment terms & late fees stated on portal
- Refund/cancellation disclosed at onboarding

5. GENERAL
- Data retention per DPDP + limitation periods, then archive/delete
- Grievance: support@lexassist.in / +91 98765 43210
- Full audit trail on every action
- Compliance with DPDP, IT Act, BCI Rules
- Amendments notified via portal
