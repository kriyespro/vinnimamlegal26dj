Hindu Marriage Act, 1955

For couples who married through Hindu rites (also covers Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs). Certificate is proof of marriage for passport, visa, bank, insurance and name change.

  • • Marriage invitation / wedding photos
  • • Age proof & address proof of both spouses
  • • Affidavit of marriage & witness statements
  • • Application to local Registrar / Municipal office

Special Marriage Act, 1954

For inter-faith, civil or court marriages. Requires 30-day public notice before solemnisation, then certificate issuance by the Marriage Officer.

  • • Notice of intended marriage (Form)
  • • Age, address & marital status affidavits
  • • Three witnesses on the day of solemnisation
  • • Certificate under Section 13
