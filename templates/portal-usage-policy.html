Portal Usage Policy

Rules for Office Staff, Partners/Advocates, and Clients using the VinnitaLegal legal services portal — access, confidentiality, conduct, and data handling under Indian law.

1. Purpose & Scope

This policy governs the use of VinnitaLegal’s legal services portal by three categories of users: Office / Back-End Staff, Partners / Advocates, and Clients / Customers. It establishes rules for access, confidentiality, conduct, and data handling to ensure professional service delivery and compliance with applicable Indian law, including:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 (and applicable IT Rules)
  • Bar Council of India Rules governing professional conduct

All users of the portal are required to read, understand, and comply with this policy as a condition of access.

2. Rules for Office / Back-End Staff

Applicable to all administrative, paralegal, and support staff with portal access.

2.1 Access & Confidentiality

  • • Staff may access only the case files, client data, and documents relevant to their assigned tasks.
  • • Client information (personal, financial, or case-related) must never be shared outside the Firm without written authorization.
  • • Login credentials are personal and must not be shared with any other person, including colleagues or supervisors.
  • • Access rights must be reviewed and revoked immediately upon a staff member’s transfer or exit from the Firm.

2.2 Conduct

  • • All client communication (calls, emails, meetings) must be documented in the relevant case file on the portal.

2.3 Data Handling

  • • Client documents must not be downloaded to personal devices, personal email, or personal cloud storage.
  • • All uploads, edits, and deletions on the portal are logged (user ID, timestamp, and action) and subject to audit.
  • • Any suspected data breach, phishing attempt, or unauthorised access must be reported immediately to the designated Compliance / IT Officer.

2.4 Escalation

  • • Any client query that requires legal advice, opinion, or a settlement decision must be escalated to the concerned Partner / Advocate and must not be answered directly by staff.

3. Rules for Partners / Advocates

Applicable to all partners, associates, and advocates handling client matters.

3.1 Case Ownership

  • • Each Partner is responsible for the cases assigned to them and must ensure timely status updates on the portal.
  • • All major documents (contracts, pleadings, notices, opinions) must be reviewed and approved by the concerned Partner before being shared with the client.

3.2 Confidentiality & Conflict of Interest

  • • Partners must disclose any potential conflict of interest before accepting a new case or client.
  • • Client information shared between Partners must be limited to a need-to-know basis.
  • • Partners must comply with the Bar Council of India Rules on client confidentiality and professional ethics.

3.3 Portal Usage

  • • Case status, hearing dates, and billing details must be updated on the portal on a regular basis.
  • • Staff-submitted drafts must be reviewed and approved or rejected within 24–48 hours.

3.4 Financial

  • • Billing structures and fee arrangements must be transparent and consistent with what has been communicated to the client.
  • • Any change in fee structure must be communicated to the client in writing / via the portal before it takes effect.

4. Rules for Clients / Customers

Applicable to all individuals and entities engaging VinnitaLegal’s services through the portal.

4.1 Access

  • • Clients are given view-only or limited access restricted to their own case documents and status; no client may view another client’s data.
  • • Clients must verify their identity (e.g., OTP or secure login) before accessing sensitive documents.

4.2 Communication

  • • All formal instructions and queries should be routed through the portal for record-keeping, rather than informal channels such as WhatsApp or personal calls alone.
  • • Standard response times will be communicated to clients (queries answered within business days).

4.3 Document Submission

  • • Clients must upload only the requested documents, in the specified formats (e.g., PDF, JPEG) and within stated file-size limits.
  • • Clients are solely responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of the documents and information they submit.

4.4 Fees & Payments

  • • Payment terms, invoicing schedules, and late-fee policies (if any) will be clearly stated on the portal.
  • • Any refund or cancellation policy applicable to the engagement will be disclosed at the time of onboarding.

5. General / Cross-Cutting Rules

Data Retention

Client data and case documents will be retained on the portal for a defined period after case closure, in line with the DPDP Act, 2023 and applicable limitation periods, after which they will be securely archived or deleted.

Grievance Redressal

A designated contact point is available for complaints or grievances raised by clients or staff: {{ site.email }} / {{ site.phone }}.

Audit Trail

Every action on the portal (upload, edit, delete, approval) is logged with a timestamp and user ID for accountability.

Compliance

VinnitaLegal and all portal users shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 (including applicable IT Rules), and the Bar Council of India Rules governing advocate conduct.

Amendments

This policy may be revised from time to time; users will be notified of material changes via the portal.
